6. Trigonometric Verification

To verify the angle is indeed 22.5°, we use the tangent function:

tan(θ) = Opposite / Adjacent = (√2 − 1) / 1
θ = arctan(√2 − 1) = arctan(0.41421356…)

To prove this identity from the half-angle formula:
