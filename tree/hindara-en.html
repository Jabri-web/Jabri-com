Al-Hindara = (Engi)neering + (Man)agement

Method: hyperd = hybrid = mixture of both styles. Top-Down (from the top of the tree) + Bottom-Up (from root to top).

Equation: Z (Geometry) + C (Management) + A (Al-Hindara) = 1

Al-Hindara is a conceptual framework for redrawing the tree from above, not for restoring its root from below. It combines the eye of the engineer and the eye of the manager into a single point.

The Proposed Framework

  • Derivation: (Engi)neering + (Man)agement = Al-Hindara
  • Method: Hyperd — a hybrid between Top-Down and Bottom-Up
  • Equation: Z + C + A = 1
