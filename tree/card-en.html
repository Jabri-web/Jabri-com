Statistical Analysis: A Two One-Sided Tests (TOST) equivalence test was used. The 90% confidence interval falls entirely within the equivalence margin, and the TOST p-value is less than α = 0.05. The result supports statistical equivalence of the mean ratio, assuming measurement accuracy and assumption validity.
Interpretation Limits: This is an exploratory analysis (not pre-registered). Equivalence applies to the mean and does not imply exact match for every tree. The small sample size (n=5) at a single location does not allow generalization as a universal geometric law; replication is recommended.
