1. Data Source and Measurement Protocol

  • Tool: Metal measuring tape (direct vertical measurement from ground level to the top of the tree).
  • H (Total Height): Vertical distance from the ground to the highest point in the crown.
  • h (First Main Branch Height): Height of the first clear split of the trunk into two main branches, each at least 30% of the original trunk diameter.
  • Sample Size: n = 5 separate mango trees at the same site. Observation independence is assumed for analysis purposes, but this assumption cannot be confirmed based on spatial separation alone.
  • Original Readings: Preserved exactly as recorded from the field, without modification.

4. Statistical Equivalence Test (TOST)

The Two One-Sided Tests (TOST) equivalence test was used to compare the mean crown ratio C = (H−h)/H against the mathematical reference value √2 − 1 ≈ 0.414214.

  • Practical Equivalence Margin: Δ = 0.03, i.e., ±0.03 in the ratio C (three percentage points). This margin represents the practically acceptable difference for equivalence testing purposes, and is not itself an estimate of measurement tool error.
  • Significance Level: α = 0.05.
  • Equivalence Interval: [0.384214, 0.444214].
  • 90% Confidence Interval: [0.404912, 0.421025].
  • Equivalence Test Value: pTOST ≈ 0.000801.

The 90% confidence interval falls entirely within the defined equivalence interval, and the TOST test value is below the chosen significance level.

Statistical Decision: We reject the null hypothesis of non-equivalence, and the results support the statistical equivalence of the mean crown ratio with the reference value within the specified equivalence margin.

Interpretation Limits: The decision expresses statistical equivalence of the mean ratio within the specified margin, and does not mean the ratio is identical in every tree or that the reference value is a universal geometric law. Given the small sample size, distributional and representativeness assumptions are difficult to assess reliably; therefore, the result should be considered preliminary evidence requiring verification with a larger sample.

5. Conclusion and Limitations

In the exploratory sample of five mango trees, the mean crown ratio (C = (H−h)/H) was approximately 0.41297 with a standard deviation of approximately 0.00845. Using the Two One-Sided Tests (TOST) equivalence test, with an equivalence margin of (Δ = 0.03) and a significance level of (α = 0.05), the results were statistically compatible with equivalence of the mean ratio to the reference value (√2 − 1 ≈ 0.414214) within the specified equivalence margin.

This conclusion is conditional on the suitability of the statistical assumptions, the accuracy of the measurements, and the justification for the chosen equivalence margin. Given the small sample size and single location, the results are insufficient to establish a general geometric law or to generalize to mango trees in other locations and conditions. Replication with a larger sample across multiple locations is recommended, documenting the measurement protocol and equivalence margin justification before collecting subsequent study data.

Note on Pre-registration: There is no dated record in this report proving that the hypothesis, protocol, and equivalence margin were registered prior to data collection. Therefore, the results are presented as an exploratory analysis, and are not claimed to be a pre-registered confirmatory test. The justification for choosing Δ = 0.03, including the practical significance of this margin and measurement precision, should be documented before use in a subsequent study.
