Tree of Unification — Visual Simulation

An interactive HTML5 Canvas simulation drawing a tree, its shadow, and a matching right-angled triangle. Objective: to show that tan(22.5°) = tan(π/8) = √2 − 1 ≈ 0.41421356.

Drawing Elements

  • The Sun at the top (source of light — Top-Down).
  • The Tree in the center: root (Bottom-Up), trunk, branches, and crown.
  • The Shadow on the ground extending a length L proportional to tan(θ).
  • A right-angled triangle below identical to the tree: vertical side = H, horizontal side = L, and the hypotenuse expresses the angle θ.
  • The θ angle arc matches above (at the crown) and below (at the base of the triangle).

Values

H = tree height (relative constant). L = shadow length = H × tan(θ). At θ = 22.5°, L/H becomes √2 − 1 = 0.41421356 (The Golden Moment).

Interactive Buttons

  • 🌐 Language toggle button (opens the Arabic version).
  • 📏 Measure button: displays H, L, and L/H numerically on the drawing.
  • 🌞 Sun motion button: animates θ between 22.5° and 60°; stopping freezes at the current angle.
  • ⟲ Reset to 22.5° button (The Golden Moment).

📐 Three levels of interpretation — do not conflate them

This page combines three distinct epistemic layers: an exact mathematical identity, a numerical visual simulation, and a pilot field study. Keeping them separate strengthens credibility and does not detract from the project's value.

Level 1 — Mathematical identity (exact)

The number √2−1 is not a choice; it is an exact consequence of the half-angle formula:

tan(22.5°) = tan(45° / 2)
= (1 − cos 45°) / sin 45°
= (1 − √2/2) / (√2/2)
= (2 − √2) / √2
= √2 − 1 ≈ 0.4142135624

This is a mathematical identity — valid regardless of any tree or shadow.

Level 2 — Numerical simulation: Pythagoras applied

The following example is independent and illustrative (at ≈ 56.54°): it shows how Pythagoras links the sides of a right triangle. The numbers are not from the current drawing (θ=22.5°); they were chosen to illustrate the application.

Z = 192, C = 290.74

Z² + C² = 192² + 290.74²
         = 36864 + 84530.74
         = 121394.74
A = √121394.74 ≈ 348.42

tan⁻¹(290.74 / 192) ≈ 56.54°

In the default simulation (θ=22.5°), the ratio L/H becomes tan(22.5°) = √2−1 automatically — see it at the top of the page.

↓

Level 3 — Pilot field study

A preliminary measurement on a small sample of Yemeni mango trees gave a mean (H−h)/H close to the theoretical value:

n_mango = 5 (out of 30 trees)
mean (H−h)/H = 0.41297
TOST p = 0.000801 (preliminary)
90% CI = [0.40491, 0.42102]

Inference limits: the sample is small; the mean indicates a numerical similarity with the mathematical identity, not proof of a general natural law for trees. Results are exploratory and require independent verification before any generalization.

Conclusion: each level serves its purpose

Z' + C' + A' = 1

where Z' = Z/(Z+C+A), C' = C/(Z+C+A), and A' = A/(Z+C+A).
Z and C are the Pythagorean legs, A is the hypotenuse. This is a normalized representation, not a direct consequence of Pythagoras alone.
