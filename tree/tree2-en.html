Tree of Unification — Visual Simulation

An interactive HTML5 Canvas simulation drawing a tree, its shadow, and a matching right-angled triangle. Objective: to prove that tan(22.5°) = tan(π/8) = √2 − 1 ≈ 0.41421356.

Drawing Elements

  • The Sun at the top (source of light — Top-Down).
  • The Tree in the center: root (Bottom-Up), trunk, branches, and crown.
  • The Shadow on the ground extending a length L proportional to tan(θ).
  • A right-angled triangle below identical to the tree: vertical side = H, horizontal side = L, and the hypotenuse expresses the angle θ.
  • The θ angle arc matches above (at the crown) and below (at the base of the triangle).

Values

H = tree height (relative constant). L = shadow length = H × tan(θ). At θ = 22.5°, L/H becomes √2 − 1 = 0.41421356 (The Golden Moment).

Interactive Buttons

  • 🌐 Language toggle button (English ↔ Arabic).
  • 📏 Measure button: displays H, L, and L/H numerically on the drawing.
  • 🌞 Sun motion button: animates θ between 22.5° and 60°.
  • ⟲ Reset to 22.5° button (The Golden Moment).
