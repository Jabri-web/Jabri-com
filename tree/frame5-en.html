5.1. Initial Form Space

We define the base function \(g(u)\) on the open domain \((0, 2\pi)\):

\[ g(u) = \left(\frac{u}{2\pi}\right)^5 \log\left(\frac{2\pi}{u}\right) \exp\left(-\frac{2\pi}{21u}\right) \]

We define the weight \(w(u) = g^2(u)\). The initial form space is:

\[ H^1_w(0,L) = \left\{ \psi \in AC_{\mathrm{loc}}(0,L) : \int_0^L w(u)(|\psi|^2+|\psi'|^2)\,du < \infty \right\} \]
Warning: The definition of \(H^1_w\) alone does not determine the closure of the form nor its associated self-adjoint extension. Identifying the intended domain for the Neumann problem requires proving closure and completeness in the energy norm, then applying the closed form representation theorem. This step is not yet completed.

5.2. Ground-State Transformation

For each function \(f = g\psi\), we define the differential operator \(A\):

\[ p(u) = \frac{g'(u)}{g(u)}, \qquad A = -\frac{d}{du} + p(u) \]
\[ Af = -g'\psi - g\psi' + p g\psi = -g\psi' \]

Thus, the quadratic form transforms to:

\[ \mathcal{Q}_F[f] = \int_0^{2\pi} |Af|^2 du = \int_0^{2\pi} w(u) |\psi'(u)|^2 du \]

Orthogonality to ground state \(g\) becomes:

\[ \int_0^{2\pi} w(u)\psi(u) \, du = 0 \]
Note: This algebraic derivation is exact inside the open domain. Operational domain and boundary conditions remain an independent step.

5.3. Self-Adjoint Operator (Formal)

The quadratic form \(\mathcal{E}[\psi] = \int w |\psi'|^2 du\) is associated with the formal differential expression:

\[ H_w \psi = -\frac{1}{w} (w \psi')' \]
Status: The differential expression above is formal until the operator domain is specified and the form is proven closed and densely defined, then the closed form representation theorem is applied to obtain the self-adjoint extension. These steps are not yet done.
