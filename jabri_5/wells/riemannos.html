📜 Executive Summary

This document proposes a conceptual framework unifying three mathematical kernels (Z, C, A) under the equation \(Z_t = Z+C+A\).

  • Z = Jabri Z Kernel: Number Theory mapped to Physics.
  • C = Jabri C Kernel: Spacetime modeled as an execution of \(Z(x)\).
  • A = Jabri A Kernel: Dark Energy linked to field zeros.
Conceptual Definition: The equation \(Z_t = Z+C+A\) is a conceptual normalization proposal, not yet a proven physical law. Deriving Einstein's equations from \(Z(x)\) requires a rigorous mathematical derivation that is currently incomplete. The variables \(Z, C, A\) currently lack formal units and domains.
