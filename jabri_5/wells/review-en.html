Updated (v7.6): The representation \(L=Q^\dagger Q\) was tested numerically on truncated domains. Results showed numerical stability and convergence of the computed eigenvalue toward zero within the studied truncation series (without constituting a proof of the zero's nature in the full operator's spectrum). High agreement was also observed between the numerical state and the envelope \(g(u)\) within the truncated domain.
✅ What is Proven (Documented Results)
  • Definition of the operator \(\mathcal{H}_g\): Documented on the open domain \((0,2\pi)\).
  • Formal factorization \(L = Q^\dagger Q\): Correct under suitable conditions (SUSY Factorization).
  • Numerical verification of state \(g(u)\): Internal verification — a stable numerical result on the truncated domain.
  • Boundary behavior: Determined that \(u=0\) is a Limit Point, while \(u=2\pi\) is a Limit Circle (requiring a Dirichlet boundary condition).
🔓 Open Problems (Not yet resolved)
  • Proving the existence of a zero ground state for the full operator: Not resolved (it could be a limit of the continuous spectrum, a numerical truncation artifact, or a result of the choice of self-adjoint extension).
  • Determining the appropriate self-adjoint extension at \(u=2\pi\): Open.
  • Proving the lower bound of the spectrum and that it is \(\ge 0\): Open.
  • Closing the full spectral problem: Not yet achieved.
⚠️ Methodological Note: The algebraic factorization alone does not prove that zero is an actual eigenvalue of the operator in the full \(L^2\) space.

FRAME 05 — Weighted Spectral Gap Strong Numerical Evidence Internal Verification
📅 Last Updated: October 30, 2026 | v5.1 | File: frame5-en.html | Status: NUMERICAL EVIDENCE STRONG — ANALYTICAL PROOF INCOMPLETE
📍 Last Status
Updated (v5.1): Ground-state transformation \(f=g\psi\) is algebraically exact. Weighted form \(\mathcal{E}[\psi]=\int w|\psi'|^2\) documented. FEM on truncated domain [ε,L-ε] (Neumann/Mean-Zero, cells [12][13]) shows grid convergence λ₁ → 1.76852571 at N=1600. This does not prove convergence to full domain (ε→0).
✅ What is Proven (Documented)
  • Definition of g(u) and w(u)=g²(u): Explicit on (0,2π).
  • Ground-state identity: \(Af = -g\psi'\) and \(\mathcal{Q}_F[f]=\int w|\psi'|^2\) — exact algebra.
  • Formal operator: \(H_w\psi = -(1/w)(w\psi')'\) — formal expression.
  • Numerical stability: Grid convergence 200→1600: 1.76858932 → 1.76852571 (Δ<1e-4).< /li>
  • Observed B values: B₋ peak 0.308029 at 4.105296, B₊ peak 0.192532 at 5.401665 (observed, not certified global max).
🔓 Open Problems
  • Certified global upper bound for B: Observed peaks are not proof of global max. Needs asymptotic expansion with explicit remainder near 0 and interval arithmetic on [δ,0.5].
  • Hardy inequality application: Applying 1/(4B) as lower bound requires proving hypotheses hold in this weighted space.
  • Self-adjoint closure: Form closure, domain identification, and representation theorem not completed.
  • Full-domain limit: Prove λ₁(ε) → λ₁(0) as ε→0.
  • Certified bound: `mp.quad(error=True)` is indicative, not interval-certified. Needs true interval arithmetic.
⚠️ Methodological Note: Formal factorization and numerical convergence on a truncated domain do not constitute a certified lower bound for the full operator.
