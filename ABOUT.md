## 📖 About This Repository

**Jabri-com** is the **official personal website** and **central gateway** to all projects in the Jabri-web ecosystem. It serves as the main entry point for researchers, collaborators, and visitors to explore:

- **The Researcher** – Profile, CV, and contact information.
- **The Projects** – Full index of all 12 repositories with live links and DOIs.
- **The Theory** – Zx function, equations, and the Al-Jabri Identity (`Z + C + A = 1`).
- **The Heritage** – Yemeni heritage (Dar Al-Hajar, Shibam, Socotra).

**Key Features:**
- 🌐 **Live Website** – Deployed on Vercel for fast global access.
- 📄 **Full Profile** – Researcher biography, education, and achievements.
- 🔗 **Project Index** – Links to all 12 repositories with DOIs.
- 🧮 **Zx Equations** – Displayed directly on the site.
- 🌍 **Bilingual** – Full Arabic and English support.
