The entire ecosystem is based on the Mother Function Zx (Repository #3). It provides a parameter-free unification (zero free parameters) between the Riemann Hypothesis and General Relativity.

  • Inputs: Riemann zeros (ζ(s)).

🔬 Research & Experimental Systems

RieOS (v1.1 and v1.2)

Experimental operating systems built for numerical verification of Riemann zeta zeros and generation of predictive cosmological models (Repositories #2, 7).

Jabri_Nobble (Repository #5)

A comprehensive collection covering all seven Millennium Problems (Riemann, P vs NP, Yang-Mills, Navier-Stokes, Birch, Hodge) using the Zx framework.

Jabri_lab (Repository #6)

A digital experimentation space containing 9 specialized projects (Jabri-5, Jabri-28, Jabri-Gravity, Jabri-Spacetime, Jabri-Darkenergy, Jabri-Planck, Jabri-Hubble, Jabri-Periodic, Jabri-all).

🌐 Infrastructure & Routing

To ensure global access (and bypass regional blocking), the ecosystem uses a three-tier routing strategy:

  • Tier 1 (GitHub): All repositories are hosted on GitHub Pages (Repositories 1-10).
  • Tier 2 (Vercel): Alternative deployment at jabri-com.vercel.app (Repository 11).
  • Tier 3 (HyperRouter): The main domain jabri.com (Repository 12) which intelligently routes visitors through GitHub, Vercel, and Google.
