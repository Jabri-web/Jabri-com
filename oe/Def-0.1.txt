Odd-only compressed structure

Nodes: V subset of {1,3,5,7,...}
Parent of odd n: P(n) = S(n)
Children of odd m: C(m) = {(m*2^k -1)/3 : k>=1, m*2^k = 1 mod 3, n>1, n != m}
Even numbers are NOT nodes in OE compressed structure.
Structural property: every node has exactly one parent, a parent may have many children.
